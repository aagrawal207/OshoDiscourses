## Included changes

- **Anonymous usage data** through TelemetryDeck SwiftSDK 2.14.2, on by default,
  with Settings > About > Share Anonymous Usage Data to turn it off. Signals,
  parameters and rules are in [docs/telemetry.md](../../telemetry.md).
- About footer, tip sheet, speech-recognition purpose string, privacy manifest,
  `PRIVACY.md`, README and store descriptions no longer say there is no tracking or
  analytics. What's New (in-app and store) names the change and the switch.

## App Privacy

Published on 2026-11-06 in App Store Connect (through the signed-in Safari session;
the `asc web` session needed a new 2FA code). Previously "Data Not Collected". Now
"Data Not Linked to You", all used for Analytics and none for tracking:

| Category | Data type | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Usage Data | Product Interaction | Feature, setting and listening signals |
| Identifiers | Device ID | The SDK's hashed per-install identifier (its privacy manifest declares it) |
| Purchases | Purchase History | `Support.purchase` and the SDK purchase preset |
| Diagnostics | Other Diagnostic Data | Playback, download and translation failure categories |

## Why

Search audit on 2026-10-06 through the iTunes Search API (an approximation of App Store
search, not a measurement of it):

| Search | US | India |
| --- | --- | --- |
| osho | 3 | 3 |
| osho talks / osho audio / osho discourses | 1 | 1 |
| osho meditation | 17 | 14 |
| ओशो, ओशो प्रवचन | not listed | not listed |
| geeta, vigyan bhairav tantra | not listed | not listed |

The app had only an en-US listing. Apple's
[localization table](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations)
lists English (U.K.) as India's first listing language, with Hindi among the others, and
English (U.K.) for most other English-speaking storefronts. Each localization adds its own
name, subtitle and 100-character keywords. Ratings were 9 in India (3.9 average) and 3 in
the US.

| Description | unchanged | en-US with British spelling | Hindi; states that the interface is English |

- en-US drops feature words people rarely search for (offline, transcript, bookmark,
  philosophy). Every keyword names catalog content, within guideline 2.3.7.
- Apple accepted the Hindi keyword field at 91 characters (213 UTF-8 bytes); the
  readback matches exactly.
- Secondary category: Music to **Health & Fitness** (primary stays Education).
- What's New covers the Rate link. Source files: [metadata](../metadata/).
- `metadata push` created the app-info localizations, and Apple created empty version
  localizations alongside them, which failed the first version create; a second push
  updated them. The repeat dry run shows no adds, updates or deletes.

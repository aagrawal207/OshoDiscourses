## Why

Search audit on 2026-10-06 through the iTunes Search API (an approximation of App Store
search, not a measurement of it):

| Search | US | India |
| --- | --- | --- |
| osho | 3 | 3 |
| osho talks / osho audio / osho discourses | 1 | 1 |
| osho meditation | 17 | 14 |
| ओशो, ओशो प्रवचन | not listed | not listed |
| geeta, vigyan bhairav tantra | not listed | not listed |

The app had only an en-US listing. Apple's
[localization table](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations)
lists English (U.K.) as India's first listing language, with Hindi among the others, and
English (U.K.) for most other English-speaking storefronts. Each localization adds its own
name, subtitle and 100-character keywords. Ratings were 9 in India (3.9 average) and 3 in
the US.

## App changes

- **Mini-player clearance.** The floating mini-player hid the last row of a scrolling
  page, for example the last discourse of a series. Each tab page now calls
  `.reservesMiniPlayerSpace()`, which adds a bottom inset equal to the measured player
  height, so it follows Dynamic Type. The height reaches pushed pages through the
  `miniPlayerClearance` environment value, because a safe-area inset on the tab does not
  pass into its NavigationStack. Covered: Home, Library, Series, Downloads (including the
  Select bar), Bookmarks, Listening Stats and History, Settings, DeNoise and its sub-pages,
  and Apple Watch settings. This replaces fixed 70 pt spacers on some pages; Series and
  phone pages in pushed stacks had none.
- **Home collections are unchanged from 1.16.0.** Build 30 showed them as large square
  covers with names below; `f8e18a6` restores the compact cards. The archive.org covers
  are waveform images, and two curated series (The Mustard Seed, A Sudden Clash of
  Thunder) have none, so large tiles added weight without information.
- **Settings > About > Rate Osho Talks** opens
  `https://apps.apple.com/app/id6774409039?action=write-review`.
- **Review prompt:** three active days instead of five. A pause from the full player or
  mini-player after 20 minutes of listening that day is now also a good moment, unless a
  sleep timer is set. Still once per version, still throttled by iOS.
- `MARKETING_VERSION` 1.16.1, build 31.

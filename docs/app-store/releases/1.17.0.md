## Included changes

- **Listen in translation.** On a series page with transcripts, the "Osho's
  original recording" menu switches to Hindi, English or any other language Apple
  Translation offers on the device. A discourse row's menu and the player's globe
  button open the same screen. Apple Translation translates the transcript on the
  device and an iOS system voice (`AVSpeechSynthesizer`) reads it. The recording
  does not need to be downloaded.
- **Translate, then play.** One screen with language, voice (with preview), pace,
  then separate Translate/Continue/Pause translating and Play actions. Settings
  fold into one line once a version exists. Osho's pace stretches pauses so each
  paragraph lasts about as long as in the recording.
- **Background behaviour (build 45 of the preview).** No lock-screen task and no
  `BGTaskSchedulerPermittedIdentifiers`. Leaving the app gets the short
  `beginBackgroundTask` grace time; playing the translation keeps the app running,
  so translation continues. Otherwise iOS stops it, and it continues from the last
  saved passage when the app is on screen again (up to three tries that save
  nothing new). A listener's own pause never resumes by itself.
- **Translations elsewhere.** Saved translations appear in Downloads (with In
  Progress rows and sizes), in Home's Continue Listening, in the mini-player and on
  the Lock Screen as "Osho · Hindi translation". Library gains a "Translated to"
  chip when one content language is selected. Switching to the recording resumes
  at the matching moment, and bookmarks from the player land on the recording.
  DeNoise, CarPlay bookmarks and the menu-bar Add Bookmark are disabled while a
  translation plays. The Watch says "iPhone is playing a translation".
- **Relaunch restore.** A cold launch puts the first downloaded Continue Listening
  recording back in the mini-player, paused, without claiming the audio session,
  Now Playing or the DeNoise tap until the first play. The position is saved on
  resign-active, background and terminate. A translation is never restored.
- **What's New screen** after each update, and Settings > About > What's New.
- Settings > About: Howzat! replaces Bruce in More Apps, and the acknowledgements
  name the sources and the independence from every Osho organisation.
- Rating and support prompts wait while a translation screen, translation playback
  or What's New is showing.

Translation listening time counts toward daily listening stats. Translated
positions never change the recording's position, history, completion or cloud
progress.

The `TRANSLATED_NARRATION` compilation condition is set in `project.yml`'s base
settings, so the feature is on in Release. Debug-only hooks (`-debugNarrationOptions`,
`-debugLowPowerMode`, `-debugWhatsNew`, `-debugPlayerDiscourse`) are absent from
the binary. `NarrationJournal` keeps the last 400 preparation events, without text,
in `Application Support/narration-journal.log` on the device.

## Store asset cleanup

### Subscription promotional images

`swift Tools/StoreAssets/subscription-images.swift` renders one 1024 × 1024 opaque
sRGB PNG (72 dpi) per yearly tier into
[`docs/app-store/subscription-images/`](../subscription-images/), with sizes and
checksums in its `manifest.json`. Each shows a lotus on still water in the launch
colour `#1E0F44` and the app's petal colours, under "YEARLY SUPPORT" and the tier
name. The lotus opens with the tier: a bud for Small, half open for Generous, full
bloom for Extra. Prices are not shown because they vary by storefront. The side
view keeps it distinct from the top-down app icon, as Apple asks, and the
lower-left corner, where Apple places the app icon, stays empty. Rendering twice gives
identical bytes.

Apple refused the version-scoped upload ("Version is not in modifiable state")
because version 1 of each subscription is `APPROVED`. The product-scoped
`POST /v1/subscriptionImages` (through `asc api`) accepted them. Apple then
created version 2 of each subscription to hold the image:

| Tier | Subscription | Image ID | MD5 (local = remote) | New version (`PREPARE_FOR_SUBMISSION`) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small (`annual5`) | `6821753721` | `42857b3b-ea10-473c-9842-dff5e84ac7e2` | `5e365bcfb8738aca70cde4a3be2c0181` | `5e683b9c-75a5-4bf9-8790-4a255f1922bf` |
| Generous (`annual10`) | `6821753662` | `3f83c146-b2d9-4300-b557-8c12261ebdae` | `f0915ed92a262b645a02dc28c7bf9958` | `9bca5243-debc-467c-b752-5f1750d3b33a` |
| Extra (`annual15`) | `6821753750` | `4b70498f-742a-4214-9832-887f98bbefb2` | `45ccde464e826011fc5d14421f844919` | `62f1aafe-5fef-4002-aed1-0623b9b2af80` |

The v1 image resource has no `assetDeliveryState`. Its `state` went from
`UPLOAD_COMPLETE` to `PREPARE_FOR_SUBMISSION`, Apple processed each image at
1024 × 1024, and every `sourceFileChecksum` matches. The products stay `APPROVED`
at level 1 with unchanged prices, localizations and review screenshots, and
version 1 stays live. An image appears on the App Store only after App Review
approves it, which needs the version-2 IDs above as `subscriptionVersions` items in
a review submission. The app-only `review submit` command below does not include
them. Without them the images stay unapproved, and nothing else changes.

It was never approved and never on sale anywhere, so no one could buy it. It is
in none of the app's ten review submissions. Apple defines `DEVELOPER_REJECTED`
as removed from review, so it may have been queued once through an older flow.
No code ever used `buymeacoffee` as a StoreKit product ID; the only uses in git
history are the web link added in `6128e4c` and removed in `74292a5`.

`asc iap delete --id 6809799061 --confirm` and a raw `DELETE /v2/inAppPurchases`
both failed with HTTP 409, "You cannot delete the in-app purchase." Apple gives no
reason. The likely cause is that the `DEVELOPER_REJECTED` version was submitted
once. Removing it from sale does not apply: it was never approved and is already
available in no territory. Completing its metadata would only make it a
ready-to-submit product, so it stays as it is. The five tips and three
subscriptions were not changed.

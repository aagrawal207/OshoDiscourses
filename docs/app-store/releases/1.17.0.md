## Included changes

- **Listen in translation.** On a series page with transcripts, the "Osho's
  original recording" menu switches to Hindi, English or any other language Apple
  Translation offers on the device. A discourse row's menu and the player's globe
  button open the same screen. Apple Translation translates the transcript on the
  device and an iOS system voice (`AVSpeechSynthesizer`) reads it. The recording
  does not need to be downloaded.
- **Translate, then play.** One screen with language, voice (with preview), pace,
  then separate Translate/Continue/Pause translating and Play actions. Settings
  fold into one line once a version exists. Osho's pace stretches pauses so each
  paragraph lasts about as long as in the recording.
- **Background behaviour (build 45 of the preview).** No lock-screen task and no
  `BGTaskSchedulerPermittedIdentifiers`. Leaving the app gets the short
  `beginBackgroundTask` grace time; playing the translation keeps the app running,
  so translation continues. Otherwise iOS stops it, and it continues from the last
  saved passage when the app is on screen again (up to three tries that save
  nothing new). A listener's own pause never resumes by itself.
- **Translations elsewhere.** Saved translations appear in Downloads (with In
  Progress rows and sizes), in Home's Continue Listening, in the mini-player and on
  the Lock Screen as "Osho · Hindi translation". Library gains a "Translated to"
  chip when one content language is selected. Switching to the recording resumes
  at the matching moment, and bookmarks from the player land on the recording.
  DeNoise, CarPlay bookmarks and the menu-bar Add Bookmark are disabled while a
  translation plays. The Watch says "iPhone is playing a translation".
- **Relaunch restore.** A cold launch puts the first downloaded Continue Listening
  recording back in the mini-player, paused, without claiming the audio session,
  Now Playing or the DeNoise tap until the first play. The position is saved on
  resign-active, background and terminate. A translation is never restored.
- **What's New screen** after each update, and Settings > About > What's New.
- Settings > About: Howzat! replaces Bruce in More Apps, and the acknowledgements
  name the sources and the independence from every Osho organisation.
- Rating and support prompts wait while a translation screen, translation playback
  or What's New is showing.

Translation listening time counts toward daily listening stats. Translated
positions never change the recording's position, history, completion or cloud
progress.

The `TRANSLATED_NARRATION` compilation condition is set in `project.yml`'s base
settings, so the feature is on in Release. Debug-only hooks (`-debugNarrationOptions`,
`-debugLowPowerMode`, `-debugWhatsNew`, `-debugPlayerDiscourse`) are absent from
the binary. `NarrationJournal` keeps the last 400 preparation events, without text,
in `Application Support/narration-journal.log` on the device.

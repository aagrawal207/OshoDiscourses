## Included changes

- Optional $5, $10 and $15 annual support subscriptions, interleaved with the
  existing one-time tips. Every feature remains free.
- Clear yearly billing labels, native subscription management and restoration,
  verified account status, and guards against duplicate subscriptions.
- A gentle, dismissible Home support card after established listening, with a
  permanent opt-out and no interruption during playback.
- A ninety-day rating cooldown, coordinated with support reminders.

`wip/translated-narration-1.16` (`17c9039866f673c874bc031383705243fbdf5f38`).

Osho Talks remains free to use, with no ads, no account and no sign-in. No demo account is needed.

NEW IN 1.17.1
Version 1.17.1 fixes a rare crash when Apple asks to download a translation language, and keeps the Lock Screen player visible while a translation is being prepared. It also adds one optional consumable tip, "Sponsor tip" (com.agraabhi.oshodiscourses.tip.sponsor), which is included in this submission.

RESPONSE TO REVIEW: Following Guideline 2.3.10, the Sponsor tip description no longer names another platform. It now reads "Funds a week of work on something new." The tip's review screenshot shows the new wording. The app binary contains no other-platform references, so build 38 is unchanged.

To review it: open Settings, scroll to About and tap Support Development. Sponsor tip is the last option in the list, below Patron tip. Like the other one-time tips, it is a repeatable consumable that unlocks no recordings, translations, features or content. A completed Apple in-app purchase shows a Thank you confirmation.

OPTIONAL SUPPORT (OTHERWISE UNCHANGED)
Settings > About > Support Development also offers the existing one-time tips and three optional yearly support subscriptions. None unlocks recordings, translations or features.

The app is an independent player for publicly available recordings and is not affiliated with any Osho organisation.

# Osho Talks Support

For questions, bug reports, download problems or help with purchases, email
**[aagrawal207@gmail.com](mailto:aagrawal207@gmail.com?subject=Osho%20Talks%20Support)**.

## What to include

- Your app version, shown in **Settings > About**.
- Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
- What happened and what you expected. A screenshot can help.
- For an audio or transcript problem, the series name, language and discourse number.
- For a purchase problem, the tip name and the error message shown.

## Downloads and playback

Downloads use Wi-Fi by default. You can check this preference under
**Settings > Player & Downloads > Download over Cellular**.

If a recording will not download or play, send the series name and discourse
number so the source file can be checked. Please also mention whether the problem
affects one recording or several.

## Optional tips and billing

Tips are optional, one-time purchases through Apple. They support app development
and unlock no features or content.

For a missing tip option or an in-app error, contact the support email above.
For billing questions or to request a refund from Apple, visit
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).
